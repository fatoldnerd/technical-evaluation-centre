# Five-minute demonstration — Brad Towers

Use the fictional Northstar Travel evaluation. Reset the demo before presenting. The reference date stays at 23 October 2026 so the overdue decision is reproducible.

## 0:00–0:45 — Start with the customer decision

“I would use this in a manager review to ask whether the customer has enough evidence to decide. Northstar has completed twelve technical activities. Its critical requirement is still unproven: authenticated booking sessions must survive the migration.”

Open the portfolio. Point to the activity callout, the critical criterion and the overdue customer decision. Open **Review Northstar**.

## 0:45–1:30 — Inspect the evidence

“The completed work is useful, but it tested anonymous routes. That cannot establish continuity for an authenticated customer. The finding is inconclusive, with an explicit limitation and no customer acceptance.”

Open the first criterion’s linked evidence. Show the result, method, environment and limitation. The validation plan is missing. The manager’s coaching question is to agree a representative customer-observed test.

## 1:30–2:45 — Record a finding and assign the customer action

Return to the critical criterion. Select **Record validation result** and enter:

- Result summary: “All 20 agreed authenticated booking cases retained the session without forced re-login.”
- Method: “Customer-observed cross-route booking test with the staging identity provider.”
- Environment: “Fictional staging, customer-configured identity provider, agreed browser matrix.”
- Date: 23 October 2026.
- Limitations: “Agreed cases and browsers only. Production configuration has not been validated.”
- Evidence support: **Yes — within stated limitations**.
- Explicit criterion finding: **Criterion met**.

Save. Point out that customer acceptance remains pending. Use **Assign an action** in Actions & ownership:

- Action: “Ruth Ellis to review authenticated-session findings and confirm acceptance.”
- Owner: “Ruth Ellis”.
- Due date: 27 October 2026.

“This is a fictional result entered for the demonstration. In a real evaluation I would require the test record and its limitations. The SA’s finding does not substitute for the customer’s review.”

## 2:45–3:30 — Show the intervention change

Open **Manager Review**. Filter to Amara Cole. The critical evidence-gap and missing-plan interventions have gone. Customer acceptance and the overdue decision remain.

“The manager can now coach the next customer decision. We have changed what is established, rather than just increasing a completion count.”

Optionally return to Northstar, record Ruth Ellis’s acceptance and update the next decision to 27 October. This closes those specific interventions. It does not record a sale.

## 3:30–4:15 — Connect the observations

Open **Field Patterns**. Show the identity-continuity observation supported by Northstar and Meridian. Follow a source link. Then show Product feedback: Kestrel has a validated gap in the evaluated configuration; Meridian has only a request.

“Two observations give us a reason to investigate a repeatable discovery question. Six fictional evaluations do not establish a market trend, and pipeline value is not lost revenue.”

## 4:15–5:00 — Make the next evaluation better

Open **Reusable Plays** and the reviewed invoice-extraction play from Vellum Logistics. Show the acceptance rubric and prerequisites. Explain the sample-based method, its limitations and the handover requirements.

“I would expect managers to help teams reuse a sound validation method while checking its applicability. A new play starts as a draft and needs review. The source evaluation remains available.”

Return to Northstar and open **Printable brief**. It brings the findings, acceptance, outstanding risks and next decision together for handover. Use the browser’s Print / save PDF action if needed.
